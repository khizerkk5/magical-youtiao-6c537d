# Classical Contrast Design System

## Vision Statement
This design system utilizes an editorial aesthetic characterized by sophisticated serif typography and a high-contrast, dark-mode color palette. It balances traditional publishing elegance with bold, functional color application to create a modern, high-readability digital environment.

## Typography
The system relies exclusively on **Newsreader**, a serif typeface designed for editorial excellence. This choice provides a coherent, academic, and authoritative tone across all levels of information hierarchy.

- **Headlines**: Rendered in Newsreader to establish a formal and structured narrative.
- **Body Content**: Utilizing Newsreader for superior legibility in long-form text, maintaining the editorial feel.
- **Labels & UI Elements**: Consistent use of Newsreader ensures that even functional micro-copy feels integrated into the overall aesthetic.

## Color Palette
Operating in **Dark Mode**, the system uses a distinctive and vibrant color strategy to guide user interaction and establish brand identity.

- **Primary Action (#068320)**: A deep, functional green used for primary call-to-actions and essential interactive states.
- **Secondary Support (#0e3279)**: A deep navy blue utilized for secondary navigation and supporting UI components.
- **Tertiary Accent (#3f1616)**: A dark, muted brown/maroon used for decorative elements and subtle highlights.
- **Neutral Base (#fff100)**: A high-visibility yellow serves as the neutral foundation for surfaces and background elements, creating a striking visual contrast against the dark environment.

## Shape and Spacing
The visual rhythm is grounded and professional, avoiding excessive ornamentation in favor of structural clarity.

- **Roundedness**: Corner radii are set to **Subtle (Level 1)**. This creates a professional look that is slightly softer than sharp geometric edges but retains a crisp, disciplined appearance.
- **Spacing**: Internal and external margins follow a **Normal (Level 2)** density, providing a standard, comfortable balance between content density and negative space.
